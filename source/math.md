# Math

## Conventions

## Expansions

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{1-x}
&=1+x+x^2 + ... \\
\ln (1+x)
&=x-\frac{1}{2}x^2 + ...
\end{aligned}
$$

## Derivatives

$$
\frac{d}{dx} \tan^{-1} (x) = \frac{1}{1+x^2}
$$

## Integrals

Gaussian Integral

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-a(x+b)^2}dx = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{a}}
$$
