# Math

## Conventions

## Expansions

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{1-x}
&=1+x+x^2 + ... \\
\ln (1+x)
&=x-\frac{1}{2}x^2 + ...
\end{aligned}
$$

## Derivatives

$$
\frac{d}{dx} \tan^{-1} (x) = \frac{1}{1+x^2}
$$

## Integrals

Gaussian Integral

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-a(x+b)^2}dx = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{a}}
$$

$$
\int d^d\vec{p} e^{-\tfrac12 \vec{p}^\dagger A \vec{p} +\vec{J}^\dagger \vec{p}}
=\sqrt{\frac{(2\pi)^d}{\det A}}e^{\tfrac12 \vec{J}^\dagger A^{-1}\vec{J}}
$$

$$
\int D\phi e^{-\frac{1}{2}\phi_x^\dagger A_{xy} \phi_y + J_x\phi_x}
\propto
\sqrt{\frac{1}{\text{Det} A}} e^{\tfrac{1}{2}J_x^\dagger A_{xy}^{-1} J_y}
$$
